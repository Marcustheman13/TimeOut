Help with the TimeOut app and website

Need help or found a bug? Send us a support request. Tell us what you were doing, what you expected and what happened, and add a screenshot if you can. Please don't include your password. We usually reply within a few days.

How TimeOut works

You get 60 betting minutes every day, reset at midnight. Win a bet and the minutes you win are added to tomorrow's phone time; lose and they come off it. There's no money involved, ever: no purchases, no deposits and no prizes.

Common questions

I didn't get the confirmation email

Check your spam and promotions folders. On the Verify your email screen, tap Resend email and open the newest email only; each new email replaces the last one's link. Once you open it, the app logs you in by itself.

I forgot my password

On the login screen, enter your email and tap Forgot password?. Open the link in the email to sign in on the TimeOut website, then set a new password in Menu → Account.

When do my bets settle?

Within a few minutes of the game ending. If a game is postponed or canceled, the bet is voided and your full stake comes back. Open bets update live in My Bets.

Why can't I place a bet?

Betting locks when you've used today's betting minutes, and it reopens at midnight. A pick also closes when its game ends or betting on it is suspended, and you'll need an internet connection to place bets.

How do I block apps on my iPhone?

Go to Menu → Screen time, turn on Enforce app limits, then tap Block apps on this iPhone, allow Screen Time access and choose your apps. When today's phone time runs out, they lock until midnight. Removing an app from the list takes effect the next day.

I really need my phone right now

Use Emergency unlock in Menu → Screen time for 15 more minutes. It costs 30 minutes from tomorrow's limit and resets your clean streak, and you get two a day.

How do I add friends or join a team?

In Social, search for a username or share your invite link. Open a team to invite friends or copy its team link. Opening someone's link adds them as a friend and, for team links, adds you to the team.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Menu → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and data. See the Privacy Policy for details.
