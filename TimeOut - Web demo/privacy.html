TIMEOUT

Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

TimeOut is a sports prediction game where you bet screen time instead of money. This policy explains what information the TimeOut iPhone app and website (marcustheman13.github.io/TimeOut) collect, how it's used, and the choices you have.

The short version: we collect only what the app needs to run your account. We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites. TimeOut never handles money.

Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address, username and password when you create an account. Passwords are stored by our account provider in hashed form; we can't see them.
  • Profile photo (optional): if you add one, it's stored with your profile and shown to other TimeOut users.
  • Activity in the app: the bets you place (picks, odds, stakes in minutes and results), your betting-minute balance, your settings (followed sports, notification and privacy choices, and the app categories you chose to lock), and teams you create or join.

Screen Time and the apps you block

On iPhone, TimeOut uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) framework to block the apps you choose when your daily phone time runs out. Apple keeps your list of chosen apps private: TimeOut only receives anonymous tokens and a count of how many apps you picked, and this stays on your device. TimeOut also reads how many minutes you've spent in those apps today, on your device, to show your remaining phone time. This usage information is not sent to our servers.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps a copy of your account data on your device so it works quickly and offline, along with your friends list, friend requests and virtual race results. Deleting the app removes this copy.

How we use information

  • To run your account, settle your bets and keep your balance, record and settings in sync between devices.
  • To show your username, photo, tier, record and team standings to friends and teammates, limited by the choices in What friends can see.
  • To let other users find you by username.
  • To send emails you ask for, such as confirming your email or resetting your password.
  • To send notifications on your device about bet results and low phone time, if you allow them.

We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't sell or rent it to anyone.

Services we rely on

  • Supabase hosts TimeOut's accounts and database (your account details, bets, balance, settings and teams).
  • ESPN provides live scores and odds. The app requests this public sports data directly from ESPN, which receives standard request information such as your IP address, but no TimeOut account information.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the TimeOut website.
  • Apple provides Screen Time, notifications and the App Store.

These providers process information only to provide their services, under their own privacy policies.

Tracking

TimeOut does not track you. We don't use advertising identifiers, third-party analytics or data brokers.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Menu → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, bets, balance, settings and team memberships from our servers and your device. You can also ask us to delete it using the contact below.

Your choices

  • Edit your username, email, password and photo in Menu → Account.
  • Choose what friends can see in Menu → What friends can see.
  • Turn notifications and Screen Time access on or off in your iPhone's Settings.

Security

Information travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and our database only lets each signed-in user read and change their own records. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

TimeOut is rated for ages 17 and older because it includes simulated betting, and it isn't directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions or requests about your privacy? Open a support request on GitHub, or see the TimeOut Support page.
